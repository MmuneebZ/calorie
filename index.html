How calories are calculated

    
calories = grams eaten ÷ 100 × calories per 100 g. Accuracy depends on the amount you enter: weighing food on a kitchen scale and choosing "grams" gives the most precise result. Built-in values are typical figures from USDA FoodData Central and the Indian Food Composition Tables; home recipes vary with oil and ghee, so use a custom food for exact recipes or packet labels.
